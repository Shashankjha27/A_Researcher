Recovery Outcomes After Adjuvant Treatment: A Pooled Re-analysis

This brief report re-examines recovery outcomes from the adjuvant
treatment programme using pooled data from two participating centres.

The treatment significantly improves patient recovery compared with
the control group.

A re-analysis found that the treatment does not improve patient
recovery compared with the control group.

Hospital records show an average reduction of one day in length of
stay among treated patients.

The pooled sample included 40 patients across the three centres, which
limits statistical power for subgroup analyses.
